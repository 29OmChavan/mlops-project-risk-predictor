Developer Notes: How I Built This Step 1 of 3

Part 1: Moving Beyond the 6-Column Kaggle Form

Most insurance cost demos ask the user to type their BMI directly and only price US plans. I replaced manual BMI entry with height (`cm`) and weight (`kg`) inputs, added 8 chronic conditions, and trained four separate country models (`USA`, `India`, `UK`, `Germany`) with Public and Private coverage tiers.

Part 2: 5-Stage Training Pipeline

The training pipeline runs stratified train/test splitting, two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov (`ks_2samp`) drift checks, interaction feature engineering (`is_obese * is_smoker`), multi-model GridSearchCV (CatBoost, XGBoost, GradientBoosting), and an R2 >= 0.84 evaluation check.

Part 3: Fixing the Uvicorn Joblib Unpickle Crash

Defining custom scikit-learn transformers inside `__main__` breaks `joblib.load()` when FastAPI workers start. Moving `RiskDomainFeatureGenerator` into the installable `src/risk_predictor` package (`pip install -e .`) solved the issue permanently.

Step-by-Step User Guide Step 1 of 3

How to Fill the Underwriting Wizard

In 1. Estimator Wizard, fill one card at a time: pick your country and age in Step 1, click Next Step to enter your height (`cm`) and weight (`kg`) in Step 2, then select any chronic conditions in Step 3 and click Calculate Estimate.

Understanding Gross Exposure vs. Out-of-Pocket

Gross Exposure is the total predicted annual medical risk. Estimated Out-of-Pocket is the remaining cost after your chosen Public or Private plan covers its percentage share.

Uploading a Lab Report Safely

When attaching a lab report in Step 3 or in 4. Lab File Parser, check the Terms & Conditions box. The server extracts disease keywords in memory, overwrites the temp file with zeros, and deletes it immediately.
