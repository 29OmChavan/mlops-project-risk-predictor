Developer Notes: How I Built This Repository project-01-risk-predictor

The classic Kaggle US Medical Cost dataset (`age`, `sex`, `bmi`, `children`, `smoker`, `region`) is a great starting point, but if you put a raw 6-field form in front of someone, they immediately hit three limitations: nobody knows their exact BMI off the top of their head, insurance pricing outside the US works completely differently because of statutory/public healthcare, and chronic illnesses like Type 2 Diabetes or hypertension aren't even in the original 6 columns.

One early bug that bit me when deploying the scikit-learn pipeline inside `uvicorn` was `AttributeError: Can't get attribute 'RiskDomainFeatureGenerator' on `. Whenever you define a custom `BaseEstimator, TransformerMixin` class inside a training script and pickle it with `joblib`, FastAPI can't unpickle it unless that class lives inside an installable package (`src/risk_predictor/components/data_transformation.py` installed via `pip install -e .`). Moving the transformer into `src/` fixed the container crash for good.

1. 5-Stage Baseline Pipeline (`src/risk_predictor/pipeline/`) Runs data ingestion (stratified split on smoker status), 2-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov (`ks_2samp`) covariate drift checks, custom interaction feature generation (`is_obese * is_smoker`, `bmi * age`), multi-model GridSearchCV (CatBoost, XGBoost, GradientBoosting, RandomForest), and an R2 >= 0.84 evaluation gate.
2. Multi-Country Engine (`country_risk_engine.py`) Trains and serves four separate country regression bundles (`usa_bundle.pkl`, `india_bundle.pkl`, `uk_bundle.pkl`, `germany_bundle.pkl`), converts `height_cm` and `weight_kg` to BMI on the fly, and models Public vs. Private insurance plan tiers.
3. Marginal Attribution & Temp Lab Parser (`explainability_agent.py`) Calculates exact marginal cost deltas against a healthy country baseline, generates "What-If" savings scenarios, and parses uploaded lab text files with immediate `0x00` byte overwrite + `os.unlink` deletion.

Help & Step-by-Step User Guide Operator Instructions

1. How to Calculate an Underwriting Estimate (Tab 1: Underwriting Calculator)

  • Step 1 (Pick a Country): Click `USA ($)`, `India (₹)`, `UK (£)`, or `Germany (€)` in the top bar of Tab 1. Notice how the regions and the Public/Private insurance dropdown update automatically for that country.
  • Step 2 (Enter Height & Weight): Type your height in centimeters (`100` to `240` cm) and weight in kilograms (`25` to `250` kg). The blue box next to them calculates your exact BMI and WHO weight category immediately as you type.
  • Step 3 (Select Lifestyle & Conditions): Choose smoking status, alcohol intake, activity level, past surgeries, and check any chronic conditions that apply.
  • Step 4 (Optional Lab Report Upload): If you attach a `.txt` or `.log` lab report, you must check the "I accept the Terms & Conditions" box. Otherwise the form will reject the upload to enforce our zero-retention rule.

2. How to Read the Output Tables

  • Total Annual Medical Exposure vs. Out-of-Pocket: Total Exposure is the gross actuarial risk predicted by the regression model. Estimated Out-of-Pocket is what the patient actually pays after their selected Public or Private plan covers its share (`oop_coverage_pct`).
  • Marginal Cost Drivers Table: Shows how much each specific factor (e.g. Smoking, BMI, Chronic Conditions, Age) added or subtracted relative to a healthy 30-year-old baseline in that country.

3. Testing the JSON API (`/docs`)

  • Click Tab 7: REST API Tester (/docs) to send live `GET /health`, `GET /metrics`, `POST /predict`, and `POST /predict-country` calls right inside your browser.
