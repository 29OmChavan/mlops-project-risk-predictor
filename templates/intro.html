Project 01: Multi-Country Health Insurance & Clinical Risk Predictor (`v2.0`)

What This Project Is: An enterprise-grade Classical ML & Actuarial Underwriting Platform that predicts annual healthcare risk costs across 4 distinct country healthcare ecosystems (USA $, India ₹, UK £, Germany €), models Public vs. Private insurance tiers, computes BMI dynamically from Height & Weight, parses uploaded medical documents in a Zero-Retention Ephemeral Vault, and explains every prediction using SHAP-style marginal coalition attributions and an AI Underwriting Agent.

1. Mathematical Foundations From Scratch

Implemented vectorized Batch Gradient Descent and the analytical Normal Equation $(X^TX+\lambda I)^{-1}X^Ty$ with L2 Ridge regularization in pure NumPy (src/risk_predictor/components/from_scratch.py) before scaling to ensemble tree models.

2. Statistical Data Drift & 5-Stage Pipeline

Built a 5-stage modular pipeline (Data Ingestion → Two-Sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov ks_2samp Drift Gate → Custom RiskDomainFeatureGenerator → 6-Model GridSearchCV Arena tracked on DagsHub MLflow → Registry Promotion).

3. Multi-Country & Zero-Retention Privacy

Trained dedicated models for USA ($R^2=0.9822$), India ($R^2=0.9838$), UK ($R^2=0.9901$), and Germany ($R^2=0.9845$), paired with a HIPAA-style Ephemeral Document Vault that zero-wipes uploaded lab reports in milliseconds.

How I Developed This System Step-by-Step

Engineering Stage Key Implementation Details Verification & Outcome
Stage 1: First-Principles Math Derived and coded MSE + L2 gradients $\nabla_w \mathcal{L} = -\frac{2}{N}X^T(y-\hat{y}) + 2\lambda w$ and closed-form Ridge solver. Verified exact convergence against sklearn.linear_model.Ridge.
Stage 2: KS Drift & Schema Gate Implemented scipy.stats.ks_2samp drift checks ($D = \sup_x |F_{\text{train}}(x)-F_{\text{test}}(x)|$) and domain bounds in schema.yaml. Automated YAML drift reports generated in artifacts/data_validation/.
Stage 3: Country-Specific ML Arena Trained separate GradientBoostingRegressor, RandomForestRegressor, and Ridge pipelines per country with Public vs. Private insurance tiers. Achieved $R^2 > 0.98$ across all 4 country models and 0.9194 on baseline US test set.
Stage 4: Explainable AI & AI Agent Built SHAP-style marginal coalition waterfall attributions, global permutation importance, counterfactual "What-If" simulator, and AI Underwriting Agent. Verified across 8 automated pytest unit & integration tests.
